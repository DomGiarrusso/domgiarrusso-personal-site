## Description

Awakened is a third-person puzzle adventure created by Lonely Tree Games. You
play as Magnol, an anthropomorphic ram who wakes in an unfamiliar world without
his memories. His search for answers leads into the mystery of Nature's Curse,
a catastrophic event that changed the planet.

Wisps accompany Magnol through the game's maps. Each one grants a supernatural
ability that opens another way to approach the environmental puzzles.

## Motivation

The team created Awakened as a senior capstone project in George Mason
University's Game Design program. It gave us the opportunity to take one game
from early planning through a finished, downloadable Windows build.

## Role

I worked as both producer and designer alongside two programmers, two artists,
and a sound designer. This project brought the planning side of a team game
production together with hands-on design work.

## Development

The team built Awakened in Unreal Engine 5, with Blender in the art workflow.
The finished Windows build is available to download from itch.io.
